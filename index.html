We’re a professional forestry mulching and land clearing company based in Defiance, Missouri, serving landowners, farms, and businesses throughout Missouri and Southern Illinois. Our crew helps you turn rough, overgrown ground into clean, usable land with as little disturbance as possible.

Common questions

How do you price jobs? ▶

We keep pricing simple and transparent. Most projects are billed using half-day and full-day rates, based on the amount of work needed and the equipment required. This allows us to complete as much as possible for you during the time you’re paying for, without the hassle of hourly charges.

  • • Half-day: Great for smaller jobs, single areas, or light clearing.
  • • Full-day: Best value for larger acreage, heavier brush, or multiple project areas.
  • • We can walk your property or review photos to recommend which option is best.

Every job is different, but this system ensures you know exactly what you're paying for—and that we can focus on getting the most done for your property.

Will mulching damage my good trees or yard? ▶

Our goal is always to protect the trees and ground you want to keep. We plan access routes ahead of time and use equipment that minimizes disturbance while still getting the work done.

Do I need to be on site while you work? ▶

It’s helpful for the initial walk-through and for a final look at the end of the job, but many clients are comfortable with us working while they’re away once the scope is clear.
